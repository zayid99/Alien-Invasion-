How to play

  1. Pick a target on Earth. Spin the globe and choose a glowing mission site in a region you have unlocked.
  2. Abduct. Each night you have 2½ minutes. Hold the tractor beam over people, animals and vehicles to pull them into your saucer.
  3. Bank your cargo. The hold fills up. Send cargo to the mothership before your shield breaks, or you lose it.
  4. Build. On the mothership, spend humans, biomass and metal on army units and saucer upgrades.
  5. Invade cities. Pick a city on the globe and launch an invasion. Destroy its shield pylons, then its defense HQ. Captured cities pay you every day.
