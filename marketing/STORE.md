## Title
Alien Invasion: Conquer Earth

## Short description (one line)
Beam up humans, cows and cars, build an alien army, and conquer every city on Earth.

## Description
You are Commander Zix of the Gray Empire, and Earth is next on the list.

Fly your flying saucer over sleepy farm towns at night and hold the tractor beam to pull up humans, cows, pigs, cars and even tanks. Chain abductions into combos for bonus loot and power up the **MEGA BEAM**. Stun the police and the army with your ray before your shield breaks, and send your cargo home before dawn.

Back on the mothership, turn your haul into an army: gray clones, hypno-brutes, attack saucers and giant mega walkers. Then pick a real city on the 3D globe, from Chicago and Paris to Tokyo and Dhaka, and invade. Your army fights on its own while you fly support, smash the shield pylons and take the city. Every captured city pays you tribute every day.

**Features**
- Real 3D Earth with 60 real cities across 29 regions to conquer
- Abduction nights in farmland, desert, tropics and snow
- Combos and the MEGA BEAM: the more you beam, the more you get
- Earth fights back: police, soldiers, tanks and missile trucks as the alert rises
- Build an alien army and upgrade your saucer on the mothership
- Daily supply drops, daily missions, a specimen catalogue and 30 achievements
- Quick 2½-minute nights, your progress saves automatically
- Plays with keyboard and mouse or touch

## Controls
- **W A S D / arrow keys**: fly
- **Space (hold)**: tractor beam
- **Left mouse (hold)**: stun ray
- **Shift**: boost
- **R**: send cargo to the mothership
- **Q / E**: turn the camera (mouse wheel zooms)
- **1–4**: drop reinforcements during an invasion
- **P / Esc**: pause · **M**: mute
- Touch: joystick plus BEAM, ZAP, BOOST and SEND buttons
