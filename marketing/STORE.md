## Title
Alien Invasion: Conquer Earth

## Short description (one line)
Beam up humans, cows and cars, build an alien army, and conquer every city on Earth.

## Description
You are Commander Zix of the Gray Empire, and Earth is next on the list.

Fly your flying saucer over sleepy farm towns at night and hold the tractor beam to pull up humans, cows, pigs, cars and even tanks. Chain abductions into combos for bonus loot and power up the **MEGA BEAM**. Stun the police and the army with your ray before your shield breaks, and send your cargo home before dawn.

Back home in your hollow Moon Base, every room is alive: grow clones in the Hybrid Lab, weld saucers in the Shipyard, upgrade your beam in the Research Lab. Turn your haul into an army: gray clones, hypno-brutes, attack saucers and giant mega walkers. Then pick a real city on the 3D globe, from Chicago and Paris to Tokyo and Dhaka, and invade. Then raid it like a general: scout the city from above, drop your troops where you want them, and fire the Orbital Laser, an EMP blast or a rally beacon. Earn up to three stars per city. Every captured city pays you tribute, as long as you keep its resistance down.

Conquer each continent's capital and its giant boss to open the next chapter, then launch the final assault on the World Defense Command.

**Features**
- Real 3D Earth with 60 real cities across 29 regions to conquer
- Abduction nights in farmland, desert, tropics and snow
- Combos and the MEGA BEAM: the more you beam, the more you get
- Earth fights back: police, soldiers, tanks and missile trucks as the alert rises
- Build your empire in a living Moon Base: train troops in the Hybrid Lab and Shipyard, research stronger troops, upgrade every room
- Night twists like the County Fair and the Tank Depot, with a 3-star grade and a personal best every night
- 4 chapters, a final assault, an ending, and New Game+
- A daily challenge night, daily supply drops, daily missions, a specimen catalogue and 34 achievements
- Giant city bosses guard the biggest capitals, one for each continent
- Earn 9 saucer skins, from the Moo-Saucer to the Conqueror
- Earth News: funny headlines that react to everything you do
- Quick 2½-minute nights, your progress saves automatically
- Plays with keyboard and mouse or touch

## Controls
- **W A S D / arrow keys**: fly
- **Space (hold)**: tractor beam
- **Left mouse (hold)**: stun ray
- **Shift**: boost
- **R**: send cargo to the mothership
- **Q / E**: turn the camera (mouse wheel zooms)
- **City raids**: drag to look around, wheel to zoom; **1–4** pick a troop, **5–7** pick a power, then tap the green zone (or the city, for powers)
- **P / Esc**: pause · **M**: mute
- Touch: joystick plus BEAM, ZAP, BOOST and SEND buttons at night; tap cards and the city in raids
